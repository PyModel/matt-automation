# My VECTOR workflow: [task]

Use this as a short take-home card. Replace the prompts with the person's answers. Keep it to about one page for an ordinary task; omit irrelevant details. Use plain language and distinguish suggestions from agreed choices. The starter instruction can sit below the card. Number the checks (V1, V2…) and the order rows (O1, O2…) so notes and later runs can cite them.

**Status:** [Draft, not signed off | Signed off by (owner) on (date)]

## V: What a good result looks like

**Result:** [What we want AI to help produce.]

**How we check:** [One to three specific checks, the source or example to compare with, and who checks.]

## E: How I do it today

[The current process in a few short steps. Mark suggestions if this is a new task.]

**Effort today, if known:** [Reported estimate or actual measurement; otherwise leave unknown.]

## C: What to give AI

- [Relevant source material and which date/version matters.]
- [Purpose, audience, and important rules.]
- [A good example or preferred format.]
- [Anything still missing and how to handle it.]

## T: Who is responsible

**AI can do:** [Actions already allowed, within their agreed scope.]

**AI prepares; a person checks:** [Work requiring review and who reviews it.]

**A person does:** [Decisions or actions kept with a person.]

## O: Our new order

| # | Who | What they do | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| O1 | Person or AI | Clear action using the relevant information | Result to hand over, check, or reason to return to a person |

Use usually three to seven rows. Include what happens when an important fact is missing or a check fails. Do not invent access to a tool or permission to act.

## R: What we keep for next time

**Keep:** [Accepted instructions, examples, preferences, and confirmed corrections.]

**Where and who:** [Approved location and person responsible for keeping it current.]

**Refresh when:** [A source changes, a lesson is confirmed, or a date expires.]

**Still unfinished:** [Only the temporary details needed to pick this task up again.]

**Saved status:** [Actually saved, not saved, or no persistent place available.]

## First try

**Try it on:** [One small example or manageable batch.]

**Owner and timing:** [Who does the next step and the agreed date, or a clearly suggested date.]

**Review afterward:** Did the result meet our checks? What did the person correct? Was the review effort worthwhile? What should change next time?

**Next action:** [One concrete step, plus any genuinely missing information or permission.]

## A starter instruction to reuse

“Help me [task] for [audience/purpose]. Use [source material] and follow [rules/example]. Produce [result and format]. Check [the agreed checks] against [source/example]. You may [allowed actions]. Return [specified decisions/actions] to [person] for review. If [important information is missing], [agreed next step]. Use these notes from previous runs only where they still apply: [reusable notes].”

Fill this with the actual agreement. Keep unknowns as items to supply, and do not turn a proposed permission into a granted one.
